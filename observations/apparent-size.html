Why does something far away sometimes seem to have a measurable size in centimetres?

00 / OBSERVATION

The thing I noticed

Sometimes, when I look at something far away, I instinctively feel as though its size from where I am is a few centimetres. I know those centimetres cannot describe the object's physical size, but the measurement still feels strangely intuitive.

01 / EXPLANATION

Apparent size

What I am actually perceiving is an angular size: the angle an object occupies in my visual field. Somehow, my mind seems to translate that angular quantity into an imagined linear measurement, as though the visual field itself were a surface on which I could place a ruler.
