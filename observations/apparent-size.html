Why does something far away sometimes seem to have a measurable size in centimetres?

00 / OBSERVATION

Sometimes, when I look at something far away, I instinctively feel as though its size from where I am is a few centimetres.

I know those centimetres cannot describe the object's physical size. What I am really perceiving is its apparent size: the angle it occupies in my visual field. Somehow, though, my mind seems to translate that angular quantity into an imagined linear measurement.
